Overthinking Loop Breaker

Thinking deeply is useful until your mind starts replaying past moments, spiraling into “what-ifs,” or stalling on decisions. This tool helps you catch the loop, check what is actually in your control, weigh realistic outcomes, and timebox one small next action.

1. Name the Loop

Spot whether you're stuck in a past replay, a “what-if” spiral, or decision paralysis.

2. Control Check

Separate steps you can take today from outcomes outside your control and park extra worries.

3. Reality & Timebox

Compare worst, best, and most likely outcomes, set a timer, and move to a small action.

Designed for fear of social scrutiny, judgment, or avoidance in everyday interactions. Each week guides you through a check-in, psychoeducation and trigger mapping, thought restructuring, and gradual real-world experiments.
