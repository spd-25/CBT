What is Social Anxiety?

Social anxiety is much more than ordinary shyness. It is an intense, persistent fear of being watched, judged, or scrutinized by others in social or performance situations. It often leads to physical alarm symptoms (racing heart, trembling, blushing) and a strong urge to avoid trigger events entirely.

1. The Trigger

Entering a meeting, speaking up, or attending a gathering where attention is on you.

2. Automatic Thoughts

Instant negative assumptions like "Everyone thinks I'm awkward" or "I'm going to humiliate myself."

3. Avoidance Loop

Escaping or hiding safety behaviors that temporarily relieve anxiety but reinforce long-term fear.

What is iCBT?

Internet-delivered Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (iCBT) translates clinically validated CBT principles into an interactive digital format. Research shows that structured cognitive restructuring and gradual behavioral experiments are just as effective when self-guided as they are in traditional clinical settings.
