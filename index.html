Overthinking Loop Breaker

Built for late-night replays, future “what-if” spirals, and decision paralysis. Instead of chewing on the same thought expecting a different answer, this tool helps you interrupt the spin and move back into real life.

1. Catch & Name the Loop

Put words on what is spinning and classify whether it is a Past Replay, a “What-If” Spiral, or Decision Paralysis.

2. Check Control & Reality

Separate what is actionable from what is out of your hands, park extra worries, and compare worst, best, and most likely outcomes.

3. Timebox & Take Action

Use a 5, 10, or 15-minute boundary timer to close the mental tab and commit to one concrete, doable next step.

8-Week Social Anxiety Roadmap

A structured, self-guided iCBT workbook designed for fear of scrutiny, negative evaluation, and social avoidance. Work steadily through unlocked weekly sessions to challenge anxious predictions and drop safety behaviors.

1. Weekly Check-in & Mapping

Track your baseline distress across social scenarios and map the specific triggers and physical responses that keep anxiety active.

2. Thought Restructuring

Spot cognitive traps like mind reading and catastrophizing, then weigh objective evidence for and against automatic thoughts.

3. Gradual Real-World Experiments

Build a SUDs exposure ladder, drop hidden safety shields, and test feared social situations step by step at your own pace.
